# Decision-model evaluation

The code for this evaluation is not public yet. It joins this repository later.

## What we evaluate

We compare language models on internal tasks taken from our own work.

## Why

General leaderboards do not tell us how a model handles our documents and rules. A comparison on internal tasks does. The comparison informs the choice of model. People still decide.

## Status

The comparison exists internally. No code, data or results here yet. We will publish the method, not the internal tasks or scores.
